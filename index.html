Painting solutions that elevate the character of your home

Mainsail Painting is a locally owned painting company for homes on Cape Cod and the South Shore of Massachusetts. We take on interior painting, exterior painting and kitchen cabinet refinishing, plus decks, wood staining, wallpaper and sheet rock. Every job starts with a visit and a written quote, and we protect the house and leave it clean at the end of each day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Another question? Call or text Vinny at (508) 593-1087.

01How soon can you come out?

We schedule your visit within 24 hours and send a written, detailed quote within one business day.

02Do you work on historic homes?

Yes. Older trim, windows and siding need more prep and more care, and we plan the job around them.

03Can you refinish my cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes. We refinish the cabinetry you have with finishes made for cabinets, by brush or spray, in satin or matte. It takes days, not weeks, with no demolition.

04Will my home be protected?

Floors, furniture and cabinet interiors are covered before we start, and we clean up at the end of every day.

05Which areas do you serve?

Cape Cod and the South Shore of Massachusetts. Send the request with your ZIP code and we'll let you know.
