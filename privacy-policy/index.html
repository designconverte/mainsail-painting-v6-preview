Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Mainsail Painting LLC ("Mainsail Painting", "we", "us"), Cape Cod & South Shore, Massachusetts. This policy explains what information we collect when you use mainsailpainting.com or request an estimate, how we use it and the choices you have. Questions: contact email to be confirmed or (508) 593-1087.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out our estimate or contact form we collect:

  • your ZIP code, the service you are interested in and any project details you write;
  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • your choice about receiving text messages, if our form offers it.

Information collected automatically

  • your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit and the page that sent you to us;
  • campaign information in the link you clicked (for example utm parameters and ad click identifiers);
  • information collected by cookies and similar technologies from the analytics and advertising tools we use: Google Tag Manager and the measurement and advertising tags it loads, such as Google Analytics and Google Ads.

Records of your consent

If you agree to receive text messages, we keep a record of that choice: the date and time, the version of the consent text you saw, the page where you gave it and your IP address.

How we use your information

  • to answer your estimate request, schedule visits and talk with you about your project;
  • to send you text messages, only if you agreed to receive them (see "Text messages" below);
  • to understand how visitors use our website and to measure our advertising;
  • to protect our website against spam, fraud and abuse;
  • to comply with the law.

How we share information

We share personal information only with:

  • service providers that work for us, such as website hosting, email, our customer list or CRM and the analytics and advertising tools listed above, and only so they can provide those services;
  • authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights and the safety of others;
  • a buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganized.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages from Mainsail Painting LLC at (508) 593-1087, we use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to: your request and project, including scheduling and updates. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The sharing described in "How we share information" excludes this information.

Full terms of our text message program: Terms of Use.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also limit personalized ads through the settings of each advertising platform: Google Ad Settings. To stop text messages, reply STOP at any time. To stop emails, use the link in the email or write to contact email to be confirmed.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep your information for 24 months after our last contact, or longer when the law requires. We use reasonable measures to protect it, such as encrypted connections and restricted access, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the laws of Massachusetts, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Write to contact email to be confirmed; we may need to verify your identity before answering.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Questions or requests: Mainsail Painting LLC, Cape Cod & South Shore, Massachusetts, contact email to be confirmed, (508) 593-1087.
