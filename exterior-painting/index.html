Exterior Painting on Cape Cod for Homes That Deserve More Than Curb Appeal

At Mainsail Painting, we specialize in high-end exterior painting services designed for homeowners who expect longevity, cleanliness, and refinement. Whether your home overlooks the shore or sits tucked within the trees, our process is tailored to the unique surfaces, textures, and conditions of Cape Cod. We protect your investment, and enhance its presence, with every stroke.

  • Weather-Resistant Finishes

    Our premium coatings shield your home from Cape Cod’s coastal humidity, salt air, and seasonal changes.

  • Architectural Sensitivity

    We honor traditional New England character and modern design alike with finishes that feel native to your home.

  • Longevity Built In

    We don’t rush. Every surface is prepped, primed, and painted with the intention of lasting beauty and reduced maintenance.

Trusted by Cape Cod’s Most Discerning Homeowners

Our clients include architects, designers, and families who understand the value of subtle beauty and a job done right. We work on oceanfront properties, historic homes, quiet retreats, and custom-built residences, always with discretion and consistency.

Our Exterior Painting Process

  1. Walkthrough & Consultation

    We visit within 24 hours and walk the house with you, looking at every surface and its condition. Your written quote follows within one business day.

  2. Application

    Every surface is prepped, primed and painted with coatings chosen for the coast. We don’t rush.

  3. Cleanup & Review

    The site is picked up at the end of each day, and when the job is done you walk the house with your project manager. Anything you point out is addressed.

Our exterior painting process goes beyond color: it preserves, protects, and elevates the architecture of your home, quietly and confidently.

Exterior Painting FAQ

Another question? Call or text Vinny at (508) 593-1087.

01Why do Cape Cod exteriors need a different approach?

Coastal humidity, salt air and seasonal changes are hard on paint. We use coatings chosen for those conditions and prep every surface before it is painted.

02Do you work on historic homes?

Yes. Older trim, windows and siding need more prep and more care, and we plan the job around them, honoring traditional New England character.

03What kinds of homes do you paint?

Oceanfront properties, historic homes, quiet retreats and custom-built residences. Our clients include architects, designers and families.

04How do you prepare the surfaces?

We don’t rush. Every surface is prepped and primed before it is painted, with lasting beauty and reduced maintenance in mind.

05Do you also refinish decks and stain natural wood?

Yes. See deck refinishing and wood staining; both can be part of the same exterior project.

06What happens at the end of the job?

You walk the house with your project manager, and any concern you point out is addressed.

07How soon can you come out?

We schedule your visit within 24 hours and send a written, detailed quote within one business day.

08Which areas do you serve?

Cape Cod and the South Shore of Massachusetts. Send the request with your ZIP code and we’ll let you know.
